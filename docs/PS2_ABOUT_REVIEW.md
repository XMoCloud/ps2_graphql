# About, typography and optical glass review

15 November 2026.

The live [imsaud.me](https://www.imsaud.me/) About view, stylesheet and cube transforms were inspected. The reference serves Helvetica Medium at normal CSS weight, rather than the system Helvetica/Arial fallback previously used here. Its exact 34.5 kB font file is now hosted locally. Primary labels use synthesized bold (700), while descriptions keep the medium face. Gray date/clock and memory-browser labels retain the black outlines, yellow selection and blue hover feedback. Logout and the fixed bottom-left Back control have no box.

The bottom-right button uses the existing PlayStation logo in black on a solid purple circle. It is portaled to the document body, with a stable scrollbar gutter, so its viewport position is identical across all four menus and while scrolling. Hover changes the circle color without a label or navigation. Clicking opens `/about`, replacing the account interface with an introduction, the user's clickable osu! web project and Discord handle `xmo2`. Copying the handle provides feedback; if clipboard permission is unavailable, the handle remains readable. Back, Escape and Backspace return to the originating account menu. Account ambience continues through About.

Five refractive glass cubes share the existing PlayCanvas renderer and persistent scene clock. The supplied PS2 configuration screenshot sets their staggered positions, including the center cube beside the orbital lights. Cube silhouettes are 15% smaller than the first five-cube pass; the reference positions are retained. Each tumbles about different axes with a period of 11, 12, 13, 14 or 16 seconds. Opening About preserves the account camera, crystal anchor and original crystal scale on desktop and phones. Cubes occupy a separate foreground depth plane; clearance is checked against the actual OBJ's rotating bounding envelope. Their projected bounds remain separate across the sampled rotations on phones through ultrawide screens. Size compensation prevents oblique ultrawide rays from enlarging the cubes again. About retains the ordinary purple smoke brightness and has no dark overlay. Body text, the osu! web link and Discord share one font size, with balanced paragraph wrapping.

Cubes ease from zero to full size over 600 ms and shrink over 450 ms before the route returns. Text fades share these timing constants. Closing during entry shrinks from the current scale without first popping to full size. A 60 fps update window covers entry and exit; idle rendering remains capped at 30 fps. The render deadline accumulates across frames so small requestAnimationFrame timing variations do not repeatedly drop alternate frames. Reduced motion skips these transitions. The refraction capture graph and render targets are retained instead of rebuilding the HDR/MSAA pipeline on every menu entry. Its color-copy pass is disabled in the ordinary account menus. No second renderer is introduced.

The original menu video was sampled at 0.1, 2.1, 4.1, 6.1, 8.1 and 10.1 seconds. Crystal shading separates violet absorption from neutral specular light, allowing dark interiors, cyan faces and blue-white cap/bevel flashes to change with rotation. Seven world-space orbital light positions drive reflected and refracted highlights on both crystals and About cubes. Orbital reflections use a soft highlight shoulder to preserve detail when lights converge.

The previous About refraction capture omitted transparent crystals and orbital lights: cube faces sampled fog alone and read as flat blue boxes. In About, these background objects now render before the existing scene-color capture, with cubes drawn afterward. Entry/exit changes their layer membership without rebuilding the render pipeline or drawing objects twice. Rays trace the cube's entrance and exit faces, including one internal reflection, before sampling the captured background. Small chromatic offsets avoid large rainbow fringes. Reflections share the crystals' already-loaded HDR environment, so face and bevel highlights change with rotation. The normal login/startup shader path remains unchanged. This is screen-space refraction, not full-scene ray tracing.

## Verification

66 tests and the production build pass. Browser checks cover 1589 × 1175 reference proportions, 1600 × 1000 desktop and 390 × 844 phone layouts, entry/exit and reduced motion. The About scene contains five cubes, twelve crystals and seven clock trails, with crystal scale fixed at 1. Camera coordinates and projected crystal center stayed identical throughout About. In the local timing capture, entry reached approximately half scale at 314 ms and full scale at 614 ms; text opacity followed the same timing. Exit was approximately half scale at 230 ms, then completed before the account route returned around 469 ms. Header PS2/Reboot01 branding, username, date and clock all resolve to the same gray, rgb(160, 160, 160). Previous checks cover six material rotation angles, login shader rendering, keyboard return, clipboard feedback, identical corner-button coordinates across all four desktop menus and 20 px phone body/link/Discord text. No JavaScript or shader compilation errors were reported. Project-detail Back remains fixed at the bottom-left with a transparent background and zero border.
